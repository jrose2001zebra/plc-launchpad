# PLC Planning Templates and Their Fields: A Catalog of Real Templates and the Core Fields They Share

Method note: All Solution Tree and AllThingsPLC templates below were downloaded as PDFs and their text extracted (pdftotext), so field names are verbatim unless marked "paraphrased". District templates hosted elsewhere could only be seen through search snippets and are marked that way. Old `allthingsplc.secure.agroup.com/files/uploads/...` links (many still appear in search results) now return **404**. The current AllThingsPLC tools live at `allthingsplc.info/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/...`. `dese.ade.arkansas.gov` could not be resolved from this session, so Arkansas content comes from search snippets only.

- MIH = *Make It Happen: Coaching With the Four Critical Questions of PLCs at Work* (Bailey & Jakicic, 2019)

## Q1. Catalog: for each template, the name, publisher, link, intended use, and fields

### Takeaway
About 40 real templates were catalogued across 9 template types. Solution Tree publishes many small, single-purpose reproducibles: one each for norms, agenda, essential standards, unwrapping, unit plan, CFA plan, data protocol, SMART goal and intervention response. Districts usually combine these into one "unit plan" or "meeting notes" form organized by the four critical questions.

### Cited Findings

#### A. Essential / priority standards charts
- **Essential Standards Chart** (Solution Tree, TARTI; adapted from Buffum, Mattos & Weber 2012). Header: Grade | Subject | Semester | Team Members, under the title "What Is It We Expect Students to Learn?" Columns, verbatim:
  - **Description of Standard** ("What is the essential standard to be learned? Describe in student-friendly vocabulary.")
  - **Example of Rigor** ("What does proficient student work look like?")
  - **Prerequisite Skills** ("What prior knowledge, skills, or vocabulary are needed…")
  - **When Taught?**
  - **Common Summative Assessment** ("What assessments will be used to measure student mastery?")
  - **Extension Standards** ("What will we do when students have already learned this standard?")
  - Instruction: "apply the criteria of endurance, leverage, and readiness… Remember, less is more… Complete this chart by the second or third week of each instructional period."
  - [Source](https://cloudfront-s3.solutiontree.com/pdfs/Reproducibles_TARTI/essentialstandardschart.pdf)
- **Figure 3.3: Chart for Identifying Essential Standards** (Solution Tree, *The PLC Dashboard*, 2025). Columns: Priority Standard | Readiness | Endurance | Assessed | Leverage. These are the "REAL" criteria. 1 page. — [Source](https://cloudfront-s3.solutiontree.com/pdfs/Reproducibles_PLCD/figure33chartforidentifyingessentialstandards.pdf)
- **Identifying Essential Standards Protocol** (Solution Tree, CTPBPLC; adapted from Ainsworth 2004). A 7-step process with "Anticipated Timeline". The criteria are endurance, leverage, readiness, and "needed for high-stakes assessments". The steps run from independent review to consensus, then a data check, a check against state/district documents, and vertical alignment. — [Source](https://cloudfront-s3.solutiontree.com/pdfs/Reproducibles_CTPBPLC/identifyingessentialstandardsprotocol.pdf)
- **Curriculum Map** (Solution Tree 2007, on AllThingsPLC). Header: Grade | Subject. Columns: Standards Taught | Curricular Big Ideas | Essential Questions | Possible Assessments | Resources | Timeframe | Loss of Time. — [Source](https://allthingsplc.info/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/CurriculumMap.pdf)
- MIH also publishes essential-standards pacing-guide templates: Figure 2.5–2.8, "Generic Essential Standards Pacing Guide". These are listed on the page but were not opened. — [Source](https://www.solutiontree.com/free-resources/plcbooks/mih)

#### B. Unwrapping / unpacking standards and learning targets
- **Unwrapping Standards Template** (Solution Tree, CTPBPLC). Header: "Standard (or standards) to be unwrapped". Grid columns: Guiding Question | Learning Targets | Depth of Knowledge Level | Assessment Ideas. The four guiding-question rows ask:
  - "What concepts, big ideas, definitions, or information will students need to know?"
  - "What smaller skills or subskills will students need to do?"
  - "What academic language or vocabulary…?"
  - "What context or stimulus (text, problem type, or situation) will students encounter?"
  - [Source](https://cloudfront-s3.solutiontree.com/pdfs/Reproducibles_CTPBPLC/unwrappingstandardstemplate.pdf)
- **Deconstructing Standards** (Solution Tree, TARTI). Fields: Essential standards. Types: knowledge, reasoning, performance skills, or product. Columns: Knowledge Targets | Reasoning Targets | Performance Skills Targets | Product Targets. — [Source](https://cloudfront-s3.solutiontree.com/pdfs/Reproducibles_TARTI/deconstructingstandards.pdf)
- **Figure 3.4: Essential Standards Unpacking Template** (*The PLC Dashboard*, 2025). Six steps:
  1. Write the essential standard
  2. Prerequisite skills + vocabulary
  3. Why the standard is relevant for students
  4. "I can" statements + rigor (DOK), "Use the same verbs as in the standard"
  5. Circle the I-can statements that will be on the formative assessment, with example questions
  6. Teaching strategies
  - [Source](https://cloudfront-s3.solutiontree.com/pdfs/Reproducibles_PLCD/figure34essentialstandardsunpackingtemplate.pdf)
- **TEAMS Process Template: Deconstructing Essential Standards** (*Literacy in a PLC at Work*, 2023). 2 pages, 10 numbered fields:
  1. Label unit / Essential standard to deconstruct / All standards in this unit
  2. Standard in grade level below / above / Prerequisite skills
  3. Notes from local standards/curriculum guides
  4. Understandings from assessment blueprints and released items
  5. Nouns/content
  6. Verbs/skills
  7. Sentence frames/vocabulary stems
  8. Student-friendly "I can" statements in a learning progression
  9. Guiding questions
  10. Group I-can statements into learning goals
  - [Source](https://cloudfront-s3.solutiontree.com/pdfs/Reproducibles_LPLC/TEAMSprocesstemplatedeconstructingessentialstandards.pdf)

#### C. Unit planning templates (four critical questions / five-step)
- **Five-Step Unit-Planning Template** (Solution Tree, CTPBPLC; adapted from Bailey & Jakicic 2019, *Make It Happen*). **3 pages.** Organized by critical question:
  - CQ1, Step 1: "Essential standards taught in this unit:" / "Supporting standards taught in this unit:"
  - CQ2, Step 2: evidence of the end in mind (end-of-unit measure): task, level of proficiency, stimulus
  - Step 3: Learning Targets grid with rows "What should students know?", "What should students be able to do?" and "What academic language or vocabulary…?", plus "Big idea:" and an "Assessment Items" column
  - Step 4: targets to assess commonly and formatively, plus brief aligned items and the expected proficiency
  - Step 5: "Sequential Plan for Providing Unit Instruction and Monitoring Learning". Columns: Days (Progression) | Lessons or Activities ("What learning targets will we teach? How will we teach them?") | Embedded Assessment Checkpoints ("What are the formative and summative assessment checkpoints?"), then "Notes:"
  - [Source](https://cloudfront-s3.solutiontree.com/pdfs/Reproducibles_CTPBPLC/five-stepunit-planningtemplate.pdf)
- **Essential Standards Unit Plan** (Solution Tree, TARTI). 1 page. Fields:
  - Essential standard, with checkboxes Knowledge / Reasoning / Performance skills / Product
  - End-of-unit assessment; When taught; Instructional days needed
  - Columns: Knowledge | Reasoning | Performance Skills | Product Targets
  - Student-friendly learning targets
  - Assessment table, numbered 1–3, with columns Assessment ("Which target… How will the assessment be used? Is it a common or individual assessment?") | Connection to Standard | Student Involvement | Time Line
  - [Source](https://cloudfront-s3.solutiontree.com/pdfs/Reproducibles_TARTI/essentialstandardsunitplan.pdf)
- **Figure 1.2: Mathematics Unit Planner** (*Mathematics Unit Planning in a PLC at Work PreK–2*). **2 pages.**
  - Header: Unit | Start Date | End Date | Total Number of Days
  - Rows: Essential Learning Standards | Prior Knowledge | Vocabulary and Notations | Possible Resources or Activities | Tools and Technology | Reflection and Notes ("After the unit, reflect and list what to do again, revise, or change")
  - Page 2: a Unit Calendar grid of Week 1–5 × Mon–Fri
  - [Source](https://cloudfront-s3.solutiontree.com/pdfs/Reproducibles_MUP_PreK-2/figure1.2mathematicsunitplanner.pdf)
  - The companion "Team Checklist and Questions" (4 pp) says to "Create three to six essential learning standards for a unit". It also says to set start/end dates, set dates for common mid-unit and end-of-unit assessments and the dates the team will analyze data, and to "Consider building in a flex day…" — [Source](https://cloudfront-s3.solutiontree.com/pdfs/Reproducibles_MUP68/appendixbteamchecklistandquestionsformathematicsunitplanning.pdf)
- **Design in Five Unit Template** (Solution Tree, HDF). **5 pages.**
  - Header: Unit Title, Beginning/End of Unit, Number of Instructional Days
  - Phase 1: Choosing standards; Essential questions
  - Phase 2: Learning targets ("I can"); Learning Ladder Progression | This Means… | Instructional Strategies (rows Extensions / Grade-Level Standards / Prior-Knowledge / Prerequisite skills); Vocabulary
  - Phase 3: Assessment plan, with rows Learning targets | Items | Assessment methods | Points and rubrics ("Administer common formative assessments every five to ten instructional days")
  - Phase 4: End-of-unit assessment link and date; CFA link plus when to analyze and respond; Common preassessment
  - Phase 5: Scoring details, Scoring dates, Student investment, Student reflection
  - Then an Instructional Unit Mapping and Calendar
  - [Source](https://cloudfront-s3.solutiontree.com/pdfs/Reproducibles_HDF/designinfiveunittemplate.pdf)
- **Course Essential Outcomes/Power Standards Planner** (Lebanon HS PLC Manual, Duncan/Kappelmann 2011, hosted on AllThingsPLC). One sheet per unit, built on all four questions:
  - "PART ONE: What do we want students to learn?": SMART GOAL; columns Essential Outcome/Power Standard | Scaffolded Daily Objectives | Anchoring and Suggested Texts | Academic Vocabulary | Processes/Strategies | High-Level Questions/Writing Prompts
  - "PART TWO: How will we know…?": Date of Common Assessment; Assessment/Rubric/Criteria
  - PART THREE: Interventions
  - "PART FOUR: What will we do when they already know it?": Advanced Instruction/Enrichment
  - The manual also includes a Unit Plan Rubric that scores each Part (e.g., the intervention must be "a different approach and not a repeat of the same activity")
  - [Source](https://allthingsplc.info/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/LHS_PLC_Manual-1.pdf)
- **Protocol for Team Analysis Prior to Teaching a Unit** (AllThingsPLC). Fields:
  - I. Review of the prior year's common assessment: (a) areas students struggled; (b) primary cause; (c) action plan; (d) unit SMART goal fill-in: "Last year ___% … SMART Goal: At least ___% of this year's students will meet or exceed our proficiency target on our team's CFA…"
  - II. Prerequisite skills/vocabulary, plus the plan to gather evidence and address gaps
  - III. "The administration could help us achieve our goal by:"
  - [Source](https://allthingsplc.info/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/ProtocolforTeamAnalysisPriortoTeachingaUnit.pdf)

#### D. CFA planning and design templates
- **Developing Assessments Protocol** (Solution Tree, CTPBPLC). 2 pages, 7 steps: Decide What to Assess; Decide How to Assess (method: selected-response / constructed-response / performance; thinking level); Develop the Assessment Plan (type and number of items per target); Determine the Timeline (administration date, data-meeting date); Write; Review; "Set Proficiency Criteria and Decide How to Gather the Data… reported back by learning target and by student." — [Source](https://cloudfront-s3.solutiontree.com/pdfs/Reproducibles_CTPBPLC/developingassessmentsprotocol.pdf)
- **Figure 3.5: Blank Assessment-Planning Template** (MIH). Columns: Content or Targets | Level of Cognitive Demand (DOK 1 Knowledge Retrieval, DOK 2 Comprehension/Application, DOK 3 Analysis, DOK 4 Synthesis/Evaluation) | Expectations for Proficiency. — [Source](https://cloudfront-s3.solutiontree.com/pdfs/Reproducibles_MIH/figure3.5blankassessmentplanningtemplate.pdf)
  - Filled example, Figure 3.8 (CFA Planning Chart for Cell Unit): Target "Explain the difference between living and nonliving things based on evidence…" → "One constructed response" → "Proficiency on the rubric". — [Source](https://cloudfront-s3.solutiontree.com/pdfs/Reproducibles_MIH/figure3.8commonformativeassessmentplanningchartforcellunit.pdf)
- **Figure 4.1: Essential Learning Target Plan for Teams** (*The PLC Dashboard*, 2025). **One page covering a whole cycle for one target**:
  - Before teaching: essential learning target (+ standard); CFA date; [Link to CFA]; success criteria ("What score = intervention and what score = extension?"); date to establish inter-rater reliability; dates for interventions and extensions in class; team SMART goal "___% proficient by ___ (end date)"
  - After the CFA: % proficient after first instruction; students needing more time and support (list or link); intervention plans; extension plans; "What instructional practices got the best results?"
  - Reflection: % proficient after the action plan; "did the team meet the SMART goal?"; most effective intervention strategies; changes to instruction / common errors / team learning; plan for students who still haven't learned

#### F. Data analysis / post-CFA protocols
- **Data Analysis Protocol** (Solution Tree, RTESSA; same form on AllThingsPLC). 1 page. Header: Team | Teacher | Date | "based on our team's common assessment of the following essential learnings". Questions, verbatim:
  1. "Which of our students need additional time and support… How will we provide that time and support?"
  2. "What is our plan to enrich and extend the learning for students who are highly proficient?"
  3. "What is an area in which my students struggled? What strategies did teammates use whose students performed well?"
  4. "What is an area in which our team's students struggled? What do we believe is the cause? What is our plan for improving the results?"
  - [Source](https://cloudfront-s3.solutiontree.com/pdfs/Reproducibles_RTESSA/data-analysis-protocol.pdf); [AllThingsPLC](https://allthingsplc.info/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/data_analysis_protocol.pdf)
- **Sample Data Analysis Protocol** (Solution Tree, HLPLCSI). Note: "Each teacher must fill in his or her data prior to the meeting". Header: Team | Teacher | Date | Common assessment | standards/targets 1–3. Tables:
  - Teachers | Strategies Used | Overall Pass Rate
  - Standard | Plan for Improving Results ("more detail than 'reteach'")
  - Student Name | Standards or Learning Targets Not Met | Plan for Intervention
  - Student Name | Standards Mastered | Plan for Enrichment
  - [Source](https://cloudfront-s3.solutiontree.com/pdfs/Reproducibles_HLPLCSI/sampledataanalysisprotocol.pdf)
- **Figure 6.4: CFA Data Analysis for a Learning Target or Standard** (Solution Tree, AA; adapted from Kramer & Schuhl 2017). Fields:
  1. Priority standard or target
  2. Count of students at the levels Advanced / Proficient / Close to Proficient / Far From Proficient
  3. Trends in student work at each level
  4. Collective plan for each group, with "Teacher:" for each group and "Date for re-evaluation" for the close/far groups
  - [Source](https://cloudfront-s3.solutiontree.com/pdfs/Reproducibles_AA/figure64commonformativeassessmentdataanalysisforalearningtargetorstandard.pdf)
- **Analyzing CFA Data Protocol** (CTPBPLC; Bailey & Jakicic 2017) and **Protocol for Using CFA Data** (SCA Table 6.2). Both are timed meeting protocols:
  1. Set the stage / review norms (2 min)
  2. Review the assessment focus: targets, which items assess each, proficiency expectation (2 min)
  3. Discuss data by target and identify students (5–10 min)
  4. Misconceptions; regroup students by specific need
  5. Instructional responses: small group vs. whole-class re-engagement; "Have each teacher share his or her original instructional strategy" (10–15 min)
  6. Items to monitor after support / reassessment
  7. Learnings for future work
  - [Source](https://cloudfront-s3.solutiontree.com/pdfs/Reproducibles_CTPBPLC/analyzingcommonformativeassessmentdataprotocol.pdf); [SCA](https://cloudfront-s3.solutiontree.com/pdfs/Reproducibles_SCA/table6.2protocolforusingcommonformativeassessmentdata.pdf)
- **Data Protocol** (Friziellie, Schmidt & Spiller 2018, AllThingsPLC). A four-quadrant grid for CQ1–4: standards and targets of focus / proficiency indicators / interventions / extension. Then "1. how does data differ classroom to classroom? (Here's what…) 2. Implications… Which instructional strategies helped students learn? (So what?) 3. So what's the plan? (Now what? Who? What? When?)" — [Source](https://allthingsplc.info/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/DataProtocol.pdf)
- **Team Protocol for Common Formative Assessment** (TARTI). Fields: 1. the assessment task (what worked / didn't / how to revise); 2. targets needing attention, which students missed which targets, which classrooms need support, "As an individual teacher, which area was my lowest…"; 3. team plan of action (assessment, curricular and instructional modifications). — [Source](https://cloudfront-s3.solutiontree.com/pdfs/Reproducibles_TARTI/teamprotocolforcommonformativeassessment.pdf)
- **NCSM Figure 4.6 Data Analysis Protocol for a Classroom Formative Assessment**: Target/Standard | Proficient | Close to Proficient | Far From Proficient, with number or names of students, patterns in student work, and how each group will re-engage. Search snippet only. — [Source](https://www.mathedleadership.org/docs/resources/essentials/Figure%204.6%20Data%20Analysis%20Protocol%20for%20a%20Classroom%20Formative%20Assessment.pdf)

### Inferences
- Solution Tree's pattern is one reproducible per task. A team doing the full cycle with official forms would fill 6–9 separate sheets: norms, SMART goal, essential standards chart, unwrapping, unit plan, CFA plan, data protocol, team response and agenda.

- No Broward or Arkansas template contents were retrievable. Arkansas DESE was unreachable from this session, and Broward's template is distributed internally.

## Q2. Which fields recur most across sources?

### Takeaway
Across the roughly 40 templates, a small core appears again and again:
- team/date header
- essential standard
- learning targets ("I can")
- common assessment, with its date and proficiency criterion
- students who need more time and support
- intervention plan

### Inferences
- **True core for one complete plan** (appears in roughly 70%+ of the relevant template types):
  1. Team, members, date
  2. Norms (short list)
  3. Essential standard(s) for the unit
  4. Learning targets in student-friendly "I can" form
  5. Common formative assessment: what it is / which targets, date given, proficiency criterion
  6. SMART goal ("__% proficient by date")
  7. Post-CFA results: students needing more time and support (names + target missed) and the intervention plan
  8. Students ready for extension and the extension plan
  9. Which teacher's strategy worked best (strategy sharing)
  10. Next steps: who / what / by when
- **Second tier** (useful, frequent but not universal): prerequisite skills, academic vocabulary, unit start/end dates, reassessment date, reflection or "what we'd change".
- **Rare / safe to omit for a minimal app**: REAL-criteria scoring, DOK grids, target typology (K/R/P/P), item blueprint counts, tools/technology, student involvement, meeting roles, administrator questions, root-cause analysis, multi-level (4–5 band) grouping.

### Gaps
- The tally was done by hand from about 40 documents. It is not a systematic sample of all districts, and most sources are Solution Tree–derived, which inflates convergence.

## Q4. Do any templates put everything for one unit on a single page or document? How long are they?

### Takeaway
Yes, several. The closest to a "whole PLC cycle on one page" is *The PLC Dashboard*'s **Essential Learning Target Plan for Teams** (1 page). It covers one target from target, CFA date and success criteria, through the SMART goal and post-CFA % proficient, students, intervention, extension and best practices, to reflection. Unit-level combined documents run 1–5 pages.

### Cited Findings
- **Essential Learning Target Plan for Teams**: **1 page**, three sections ("Information to Agree on Prior to Teaching the Unit" / "Action Plan Determined by the Team After the CFA" / "Reflections to Capture After Implementing the Action Plan"). Purpose line: "To Increase Student Learning and Capture Adult Learning." — [Source](https://cloudfront-s3.solutiontree.com/pdfs/Reproducibles_PLCD/figure41essentiallearningtargetplanforteams.pdf)
- **Essential Standards Unit Plan** (TARTI): **1 page** per essential standard: standard, end-of-unit assessment, timing, targets, I-cans, assessment timeline. It does not include the intervention response; that is a separate "Team Response" page. — [Source](https://cloudfront-s3.solutiontree.com/pdfs/Reproducibles_TARTI/essentialstandardsunitplan.pdf)
- **LHS Course Essential Outcomes Planner**: **1 page per unit**, covering all four critical questions (SMART goal, standards/objectives/vocabulary, CFA date and criteria, interventions, enrichment). A separate 1-page Intervention Grouping sheet follows. — [Source](https://allthingsplc.info/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/LHS_PLC_Manual-1.pdf)
- **Five-Step Unit-Planning Template**: **3 pages**, covering CQ1–2 and the instructional sequence. CQ3/CQ4 responses are not included. — [Source](https://cloudfront-s3.solutiontree.com/pdfs/Reproducibles_CTPBPLC/five-stepunit-planningtemplate.pdf)
- **Mathematics Unit Planner**: **2 pages** (planner + 5-week calendar). — [Source](https://cloudfront-s3.solutiontree.com/pdfs/Reproducibles_MUP_PreK-2/figure1.2mathematicsunitplanner.pdf)
- **Design in Five Unit Template**: **5 pages** (five phases + calendar); the most comprehensive unit document found. — [Source](https://cloudfront-s3.solutiontree.com/pdfs/Reproducibles_HDF/designinfiveunittemplate.pdf)
- **Data Protocol** (Friziellie et al.): 1 page that puts all four critical questions and a "Now what? Who? What? When?" plan on one sheet. It is used after data, not for planning. — [Source](https://allthingsplc.info/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/DataProtocol.pdf)
- A Google Docs district "cycle guide" breaks the cycle into weekly steps (create CFA → analyze → action plan for intervention → cycle reflection). Search snippet only; the author or district is not identified. — [Source](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1MNGYQv0eq1D4a3ueRTOuZwxHgPjgRyemoVO32luFSEk/export?format=pdf)

### Inferences
- The PLC Dashboard ELT Plan, combined with a short norms block and a unit header (standards, targets, dates), is close to a ready-made blueprint for a "one complete PLC plan" app.
- One unit = a few essential standards; Solution Tree's math guidance says three to six. Each standard runs a CFA → response loop. So the natural data model is Unit → Essential standard(s) → Target(s) → CFA → Response groups, rather than one flat form.

### Gaps
- No public Arkansas DESE or Broward one-page combined template could be verified from this session.
