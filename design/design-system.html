PLC Launchpad · design language

Color tells you where you are. Icons tell you what it is.

These rules come before any screen. Every page in Launchpad uses the same five colors, the same icons and the same way of explaining PLC words, so a teacher learns them once and can read every screen at a glance.

  • One color, one meaningBlue is always question 1. It never decorates anything else.
  • Every idea has an iconThe same drawing in the menu, on cards, in buttons and in the glossary.
  • Plain words first, PLC term secondTeach the real term without making anyone decode it.
  • Circles for the big ideasQuestions are circles, actions are capsules, help is a speech bubble.

2 · Icons for quick reference

One drawing per idea

Each icon appears wherever its idea does: the menu, card headers, buttons, the glossary and the narrated tours. Family icons take their family's color.

3 · Plain words, then the real term

Say it simply. Then name it.

Every screen leads with an everyday question. The official PLC term sits right under it in a dashed chip. Tap the chip for a short definition, an example and a narrated explanation. Teachers learn the vocabulary as they use it.

How will we know they learned it?

Plan one short check that every teacher on the team gives the same way, around the same day.

Common formative assessment

Common means every teacher on the team gives the same check. Formative means it happens during learning, while there's still time to help, not as a final grade.

Example: a 4-question exit ticket every 9th-grade class takes on Thursday.

  1. Family icon in a circle
    Shows which question you're on before you read a word.
  2. A question anyone can answer
    Written the way a teammate would ask it across the table.
  3. One sentence on what to do
    An action, not a definition.
  4. The PLC term chip
    Dashed border, family color, always labeled "PLC term". The same chip in every place a term appears.
  5. Definition, example, voice
    Short enough to read in ten seconds, with a narrated version for anyone who'd rather listen.
4 · Progress you can see

Fill the circle

Status is always a circle that fills, in the color of the question it belongs to. It works without color too: the center shows a dot, a dash or a check.
